# Conditions Treated

- Some of the conditions we have treated over the course of our practice include:


- Hormone Replacement: Preventive Medicine, Menopause, Osteoporosis, CV disease, Libido, Sleep, Energy, Cognitive Decay/Alzheimers/Dementia, Muscle Tone, Emotional Stability, Immune Function


- Cancer support: Kelly metabolic program, enzyme inhibition, hormone blockage.
