# Intake Forms

- The [Informed Consent](https://naturedox.com/InformedConsent.html) must be read, understood and signed before a patient will be treated at our clinic.


- Some of the forms below are very lengthy, allow enough time to fill them out before coming in to the clinic.


- The [General Intake](https://naturedox.com/GeneralIntake.html) and Informed Consent forms should both be filled out before coming in to be seen as a patient for a first visit.


- Each of the forms may be copied and pasted into Word, filled out on the computer, saved as a file, and [emailed](mailto:naturedox@qwest.net).


Form #1: [General Intake:](https://naturedox.com/intake1-general-intake/) Personal History, Past Medical History, Family History, Habits, Diseases, Food, Exercise…


Form #2: [Review of Systems:](https://naturedox.com/intake1-general-intake/) Symptoms in various parts of your body


Form #3: [Nutritional Assessment Intake:](https://naturedox.com/NCDHeavyMetalTest.html) How does your body respond to various stimuli?


Form #4: [Homeopathic Intake:](https://naturedox.com/intake3-homeopathic-intake/) Mental/Emotional response to life?


Form #5: [Physical Exam:](https://naturedox.com/intake4-physical-exam/) Areas of the body, how they look, sound, and function


Form #6: [Laboratory Tests:](https://naturedox.com/intake5-lab-order/) Selected tests for internal chemistry indications of health


Form #7: [Informed Consent:](https://naturedox.com/InformedConsent.html) Ethics, expectations, contracts of performance for Doctor and Patient
