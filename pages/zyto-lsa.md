# Zyto LSA

The Unit Configuration: The LSA consists of a handpiece, which measures the skin resistance of the body in response to various frequencies sent into the body from a computer through the USB port.  The handpiece thus both sends a signal/frequency to the skin of the hand, as well as measuring the skin resistance response to that signal.  The skin resistance response is computed according to a complex algorithm that is referred to as deviation units.  The skin resistance is measured at the beginning of the test cycle, and then re-measured after the test frequency has challenged the patient.  


Body Diagnosis Stage: The first pass of the LSA analysis is done to determine the body’s level of reactivity and by correlation, disturbance, with regard to a list of the common stressors.


Remedy Response Stage: Next, the Limbic System Analysis unit probes the body with frequencies associated with various classes of remedies to see the body’s response to each remedy class.


Remedy Expansion Stage: The entire list of remedies associated with a class with a high response is then run, and the remedies with the highest response are then chosen from that list to see if they balance the originally diagnosed body disturbances.


Remedy Balancing Stage: Those remedies are then sent to the body in combination with the frequency of the original disturbed body parameter.  Those remedies which balance the disturbances are kept as part of the treatment program.  Thus, the LSA is a diagnostic unit, which yields a treatment protocol.
